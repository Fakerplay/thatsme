03 / METHODOLOGY VISUAL DIRECTION & CRAFT

Strategy gives the work direction. Taste decides where it stops.

Design isn't an assembly line where ideas are passed down a conveyor belt. I work across the entire arc: uncovering what actually needs to be said, establishing the visual atmosphere, and executing the frames, identities, and interfaces myself.

01 / WORKING METHODOLOGY

How decisions actually get made.

01.01

Find the narrative tension before opening Figma.

Every brief has an official commercial requirement and an unspoken human reality. Understanding the distinction between what a business sells and what a person actually feels is what separates distinctive identities from forgettable templates.

01.02

Gather a surplus of references, then ruthlessly cut.

Anyone with internet access can assemble an endless moodboard. The real discipline of art direction is taste through subtraction: discarding the 90% that is merely trendy until only the ideas that belong to the brief remain.

01.03

Direction and hands-on craft stay in the same room.

When art direction is divorced from making, nuances disappear in translation. I stay directly inside the typography, compositing, code, and motion timelines from the first sketch through production delivery.

What is intentionally avoided
