About the project

I designed a brochure introducing Prachyam and the ideas behind its work. English and Devanagari typography sit alongside cultural collages and an illustrated timeline, with orange accents and recurring page details connecting the spreads.

What I did

  • Designed the cover and interior editorial layouts.
  • Combined cultural imagery, illustration and typography to carry the brand’s story across the brochure.
